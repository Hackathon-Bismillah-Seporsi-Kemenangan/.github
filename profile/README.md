# Bismillah Seporsi Kemenangan

**Building technology. Solving real problems. One hackathon at a time.**

We are a team of developers and technology enthusiasts
exploring the intersection of software engineering,
innovation, and real-world impact.

Born from curiosity, driven by deadlines, and powered
by the hope of bringing home a victory.

## What We Do

- Software Engineering
- Hackathon & Competitive Innovation
- Research & Experimentation
- Technology for Social Impact
- Open Source Development

## Our Philosophy

We believe technology should be more than a demonstration
of technical capability. It should solve meaningful problems,
remain accessible, and create measurable impact.

- Build with purpose.
- Keep engineering practical.
- Learn through experimentation.
- Ship, evaluate, and iterate.

## Behind the Name

**Bismillah Seporsi Kemenangan**

An Indonesian expression of hope, determination,
and a healthy appetite for victory.

Because every great project starts with a little faith,
a lot of curiosity, and probably not enough sleep.
